## Priority Issues

**[P0] Broken markup and grammar in the page's opening paragraph** (offres.njk:13–17)
- **What**: "des structures **publics**" should be "publiques"; line 16 is a stray `</p>` with no opener; line 17 has a stray `</strong>` never opened, plus "en **terme** de numérique" should be "en termes de".
- **Why it matters**: this is the first thing every prospect reads on the page selling rigor and accessibility. Invalid HTML plus spelling faults directly undercut conviction rung #2 from PRODUCT.md ("elle est vraiment compétente") before the visitor reaches anything else.
- **Fix**: rewrite as two clean `<p class="section-subtitle">` blocks; fix both agreements; reorient sentence one toward the visitor's problem rather than Alexandra's tenure.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden` (markup), then `/impeccable clarify` (copy).

**[P1] The primary conversion is buried and demoted** (offres.njk:92–104, 265–271)
- **What**: the free 30-min call exists only as an outline button inside a highlight card in the *first* category. Page-end (`#offres-contact`) offers only the contact form as a tampon (primary) button; the page then continues into a long FAQ with no CTA at all.
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md defines success as the Cal.com booking and demands "zéro friction vers le rendez-vous." The peak-end rule says the last actionable moment sells the wrong thing.
- **Fix**: make `#offres-contact` a proper closing block with the tampon `btn-primary` style on the Cal.com link ("Réserver 30 minutes gratuites"), contact form as a secondary text link; repeat a slim booking CTA after the FAQ.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify` (CTA hierarchy), then `/impeccable bolder` on the closing block.

**[P1] Filter has no accessible state, no feedback, and silently destroys content** (offres.njk:19–25, main.js:104–162)
- **What**: filter pills lack `aria-pressed`; no live region announces results; empty categories (e.g. "Sites internet" under "ESN") vanish with no explanation; filter state isn't reflected in the URL.
- **Why it matters**: a screen-reader user can't tell which filter is active or that anything happened; a sighted novice may believe offers were removed from the catalogue entirely. This is happening on a site that sells accessibility as a deliverable.
- **Fix**: add `aria-pressed="true/false"` to pills; add a visually-hidden `aria-live="polite"` status line; replace silent hiding with a one-line "Pas d'offre dédiée à ce profil — parlons-en" message; mirror the filter in `?profil=`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`.

**[P2] Heading semantics inverted** (offres.njk:12, 41, 122, 219; faq.njk:75)
- **What**: h1 → h3 with no h2 in the main flow; all seven offer titles are `<div class="offre-title">`; the FAQ's only heading is a small mono `h2.section-label` appearing after the h3s.
- **Why it matters**: heading navigation is the #1 screen-reader browsing strategy — the products actually being sold are unreachable as headings.
- **Fix**: categories → `<h2>`, offer titles → `<h3>`, give the FAQ a real VG5000 `<h2>` with the mono label as a kicker above it.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden` / `/impeccable typeset`.

**[P2] Filter labels demand supplier-side self-classification; the priority audience's content is ordered last**
- **What**: pill labels use segment jargon ("ESN & éditeurs de logiciels" means nothing outside the industry); "Sites internet" — the priority persona's offer — is the third of three sections, behind two sections of SaaS/API vocabulary; filtering to "Collectivités" hides the Sites offers entirely even though collectivités buy websites too.
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md's primary persona arrives "sans savoir précisément quoi demander" — the page's first interactive element asks them to already know their own category.
- **Fix**: reframe pills by need ("Un site internet", "Un logiciel / une appli", "Du conseil ou un atelier", "Renfort d'équipe (ESN)"), or add plain sublabels; consider reordering sections Conseil → Sites → Logiciels; add `public` to at least one sites card.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt` + `/impeccable clarify`.
